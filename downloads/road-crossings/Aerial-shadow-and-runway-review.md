# Highway shadows and the airport runway intersection

A read-only inspection of the fresh Airport Corporate and Meadowvale pictures traced the reported dark wedges to the exact saved scene surfaces and the directional light.

At the Airport Corporate Highway 401 crossing, sampled dark pixels hit restored terrain at approximately 150–152 m ASL. Sun rays from those points hit the raised pavement 4.8–6.9 m away; the visible pavement above is near-flat at approximately 155.2–155.4 m ASL. At Meadowvale, sampled dark pixels hit the protected lower road at approximately 183.8–184.5 m ASL. The upper crossing decks cast those shadows, with sampled sun-ray intersections 3.8–4.4 m away. The upper-deck point near the reported centre is approximately 187.6 m ASL and lit. These are normal shadows and views beneath the raised roads, not new road dips. No road, terrain, water, profile or lighting changes were made from this inspection.

The tiny black notch at the airport runway intersection had a different cause. The mapped 15L/33R and 05/23 runway surfaces overlapped. Two upward-facing triangles occupied exactly the same height at the black pixels, with no object blocking the sun. The original airport supplement was byte-identical to the preserved Transit Depth asset, and the black pixels were present in that earlier picture too.

The bounded repair affects only the existing `OSM-runway` primitive in the airport supplement. It splits multiply covered triangles at runway/segment intersections, keeps the higher existing source plane where the two fitted surfaces differ, and keeps one deterministic face where they coincide. It does not reroute a runway, change its mapped width, refit elevations or modify the taxiway and apron primitives. The other source vertices and untouched triangles are retained exactly.

The [repair and public-transfer checks](Airport-runway-overlap-repair.json) record 126 source triangles retessellated and 5,471 unchanged runway triangles. The serialized union footprint differs by 0.0219 m² across an approximately 891,208 m² footprint, with at most 0.176 mm boundary-vertex deviation from float32 representation. New vertices stay on existing source planes within 0.022 mm. Residual overlaps below 0.01 m² are subpixel numerical slivers, not the former large coincident faces. This is a bounded geometric repair, not a claim of survey accuracy or unchanged topology.

Both the renderer and website load this supplement only for Airport Corporate, Malton and the city overview. Their three aerial pictures and three editable scenes were regenerated; the other thirty pictures do not import this asset. The original render inputs are preserved, with the airport change documented by the [scoped amendment checks](Airfield-render-amendment-QA.json). Geometry and decoded public GLB checks passed, and native-image reinspection confirmed that the black notch disappeared without a new clipping or surface defect in the three affected views.

The refreshed [decoded public-model check](Public-asset-validation.json) covers eighty source/public pairs, including the airport supplement.
