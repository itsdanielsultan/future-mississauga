# Lakeview Village: model coverage and remaining gaps

Reviewed September 27, 2026. This revision adds **41 townhome building groups or roof envelopes**: 19 at Pier House, 17 at South Banks and five additional Aura envelopes. These are building groups, not dwelling counts. They join the earlier Harbourwalk reconstruction, ten retained Aura envelopes and 26 municipal components. **The complete village is not yet modelled.**

The [City overview](https://www.mississauga.ca/projects-and-strategies/city-projects/lakeview-waterfront-area-redevelopment/lakeview-village/) currently describes approximately **12,000 homes**; the [developer](https://mylakeviewvillage.com/project) advertises up to **11,000**. The provincial ceiling of **16,000** is a different planning quantity. None is a promise of the final built total. This edition uses current parcel-specific references instead of filling missing blocks with the linked [October 2019 masterplan](https://cdn.aglty.io/tgbxpvs6/pillars/planning/LakeviewVillage_DMP4.0.pdf).

| **Pier House** | 19 row-building groups with approximate pitched roofs, gables and balcony articulation. | All footprints lie within current **SP 25/48 W1, Block 4**. | **South Banks** | 17 numbered blocks, following the source plan's pitched roofs and flat terraces. | All footprints lie within **SP 26/2 W1**. The artist concept compresses road widths, so one whole-plan transform fits its landscaped site edge to the City parcel; buildings were not individually shifted or clipped. The roughly **12.9–13 m** maximum heights are estimated. Working allowances are ±3 m height, ±4 m position and ±2 m outline. |
| **Aura** | Five additional contextual roofs, bringing the retained source-plan coverage to **15 envelopes**. | Three eastern additions fall wholly within **SP 25/68 W1, Block 8**. Ten older envelopes fall within **SP 26/24 W1, Phase 2**. Two western grey roofs are shown in the developer plan and fit the current parent parcel, but lack a verified current application assignment. All five reuse the existing registration, with an estimated **13 m** height, ±3.5 m height, ±10 m position and ±3 m outline allowances. |

These allowances describe modelling uncertainty, not survey confidence intervals. Current City records list 164 townhouses for Block 4, 306 for South Banks, 88 for Block 8 and 264 for Aura Phase 2; the model groups are not a unit-by-unit census. The active application records do not establish construction or occupancy dates. All added completed-form geometry appears in the **future scenario only**.

## Retained municipal massing and Aquanova

The **26 municipal components** have now been compared against actual current application polygons using their projected triangles. Every assigned component is contained in its matching parcel. Components may be towers, podiums or townhouse volumes, so 26 is not a building count.

| Application | Retained IDs | Identification and remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SP 24/019** | 103-1077–103-1079 | Argo Caivan **Block 15**, at Shoreview Landing and South Shore Promenade. Three retained components include a tower; the current architectural edition is not verified. |
| **SP 24/015** | 103-1080–103-1086 | Greenpark. **Aquanova is component 103-1083**, whose footprint contains the official **950 Aerodrome Avenue** address point. It is linked in the catalogue, not added again. |
| **SP 22/056** | 103-1087–103-1094 | **Block 1 / Branthaven**, around Shorefield Lane and Leading Sea Avenue. Retained Birch-era massing remains; the newer Pier House at Block 4 does not establish its cancellation. |
| **SP 22/082** | 103-1095–103-1102 | **Block 2**, around Cove View, Leading Sea and Headwind Lane: an apartment building, six townhouse bodies and a lower component. Current description and unit fields differ; final architecture is unverified. |

The Aquanova identification uses the [official City address service](https://services6.arcgis.com/hM5ymMLbxIyWTjn2/ArcGIS/rest/services/Address/FeatureServer/0) and parcel geometry. Its source height field is **143.81 m**, not an independently confirmed final architectural height. The City application describes 43 storeys while developer marketing counts 42 floors of suites; these counts use different conventions.

**The fourth Greenpark tower remains a genuine unresolved gap.** The current application describes four buildings of 43, 26, 21 and 32 storeys, but the retained group contains only three tall bodies. Component 103-1085 is approximately 11.93 m high in the original multipatch itself, with a source height attribute of 11.08 m; its vertices and indices are unchanged in the original and reconciled models. The discrepancy is therefore present in the source, not a missing display layer. No unsupported height was stretched into a fourth tower.

## Harbourwalk and timing

The earlier Harbourwalk reconstruction remains: two **19-storey wings**, descending terraces, balcony bands and an open courtyard, registered to **SP 21/168**, approved January 15, 2025, at 910 Aerodrome Avenue / 700 Leading Sea Avenue. Its approximately **61.7 m** maximum height is estimated, with a ±6 m working allowance; placement and outline allowances are ±5 m and ±3 m. The sources are [Tridel's exterior references](https://www.tridel.com/lakeviewvillagecondos/) and its [2022 brochure](https://www.landpower.ca/static/media/Harbourwalk-Brochure.pdf), cross-checked against current imagery.

[Tridel construction updates](https://www.tridel.com/lakeviewvillagecondos/news/) establish construction, not measured present-building heights. The finished reconstruction is future-only. Current suite pages for [2K](https://www.tridel.com/condos/lakeviewvillagecondos/1678227525/) and [2M+D](https://www.tridel.com/condos/lakeviewvillagecondos/1716401810/) advertise **Fall 2028 occupancy, subject to change**. The City's early-2029 initial-occupancy expectation may refer to a different milestone. Neither establishes actual completion or the whole community's completion date.

## Still incomplete

The Innovation District's parcel architecture, the Sustainability Centre's building form, the unresolved Greenpark tower, other village blocks and final public-space details still need stronger current references. The [2022 noise report](https://www.mississauga.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/01115530/lakeview-village-environmental-noise-feasibility-report.pdf) uses historical Sasaki massing; its 2024 upload path does not make that geometry current.

Ground uses the provincial DTM and municipal planimetric outlines. Foundations meet sampled terrain, but future civil grading and the present Jim Tovey reclamation edge are not surveyed reconstructions. The [May 30, 2026 conservation-area opening](https://cvc.ca/discover-the-new-jim-tovey-lakeview-conservation-area/) is a separate milestone, not confirmation of shoreline geometry.

Developer artwork and photography are research references, not automatically licensed redistribution assets. New geometry is an independent reconstruction with attribution; no source raster is embedded as a texture. Municipal data remains subject to City terms. Private correspondence is not included.
