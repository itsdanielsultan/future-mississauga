# Absolute World model correction

The two towers now have distinct heights and twisting profiles. The earlier view combined simple tower solids with a conflicting detailed pair. That pair repeated the same elevation pattern for both towers. It was unsuitable as a representation of the completed buildings.

The correction removes 35,238 conflicting triangles wherever the full site appears. All retained source coordinates and triangle values remain exact. The shared podium retains its 216 source triangles. The original municipal datasets and the earlier complete edition remain preserved.

| Tower | Floors | Architectural height | Published twist |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 60 Absolute, south | 56 | 175.6 m | 209° total, varying by level |
| 50 Absolute, north | 50 | 157.9 m | 4° per storey, 200° total |

Heights and completion are checked against the [60 Absolute](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/absolute-world-building-d/2939) and [50 Absolute](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/wd/3918) records. The construction-document author [Arch.DWG](https://archdwg.ca/absolute-world/) confirms the different floor counts and completion in 2012. The [MAD and Dominus case study in CTBUH Journal](https://misfitsarchitecture.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/5-case-study-absolute-world-towers-mississauga.pdf) supplies the south plan outline, three rotation anchors, the different rotation methods and facade descriptions. [MAD’s project page](https://www.i-mad.com/projects/absolute-towers) confirms continuous balconies as an essential design feature.

This remains an architectural visualization. South intermediate rotations are smoothly interpolated between the documented anchors, constrained to the published 1–8° range. Footprint dimensions are fitted to municipal capture geometry; locations retain the mapped tower centres. The north plate is an ellipse fitted to the captured roof outline. Approximate plan and registration allowances are ±2 m, rather than surveyed confidence intervals. City vertical extents differ from the published architectural heights, so the retained ground datum should not be read as a certified absolute elevation.

The model includes continuous balconies, a separate glazing envelope and the mapped south roof outline. Slab thickness, guard height, glazing setbacks, typical floor/roof subdivisions and materials are illustrative. A complete set of construction elevations and facade details was not publicly recovered. The isolated close view is a quality check of these disclosed model forms; its neutral ground is not a site rendering.

Validation: 319 closed outward-facing components, 118,976 triangles, finite coordinates, exact nominal tower heights, and no retained source-tower overlaps. The model and detailed checks are supplied alongside this note.
