# Visual design review — Transit Revamp edition

The reference review used visible images on [Stephen Velasco’s Downtown Core page](https://www.stephenvelasco.com/downtown-core) and a [FutureModelTO three-stage skyline post](https://x.com/FutureModelTO/status/1447936343821078528). It does not claim access to his production settings or model files. The aim is a clear, attractive architectural model of Mississauga, with evidence attached to the buildings shown.

**Lighting makes the forms legible.** Directional sunlight, soft shadows and ambient illumination reveal setbacks and anchor towers to the ground. These are illustrative lighting choices, not a sun-position study for a specified date. Surface treatment supports the model's evidence colours; it does not establish real facade materials where those are unknown.

**Context establishes scale.** City buildings beyond each district window, Ontario terrain, streets, the Credit River, parks, woodland outlines and public-tree inventory positions make the skyline part of a recognisable place. Additional canopy instances and their sizes remain illustrative. Publicly mapped airport surfaces, street details, bridge decks and rail context improve continuity, with their individual source limitations retained.

**The new architecture fills conspicuous gaps.** M3 now reaches its verified maximum through a schematic upper contour and an approximate stepped crown silhouette. M5 has a native developer interaction proxy, clearly distinguished from verified architecture. Voya's two curved tower envelopes, Square One Block 5 T1 and the Promenade infill have been added from published plans. Cooksville and Clarkson have dated proposal envelopes. New or replacement waterfront forms include 26 Park, 50 High, 128 Lakeshore, 900 Lakeshore West, 70 Park, 170 Lakeshore missing Brightwater concept blocks and approximate Aura townhome envelopes. Mapped Lakeview docks and beach context also improve the waterfront. These additions have different levels of fidelity; attractive presentation must not flatten those differences.

**The landmark and source defects are corrected separately.** Absolute World now has two distinct source-derived twist profiles, paired heights and continuous rooftop forms while preserving the shared podium. Bough Beeches, Chelsea B4/B5/B6 and 55 Dundas add dimensioned proposal envelopes. The Chelsea layout removes two obsolete overlapping source forms and identifies the separate unresolved B3 height in violet. The Dundas replacement uses exact source-triangle separation so its existing houses remain in the existing view and disappear only in the future proposal view; the retained heritage building remains.

**Underground artifacts have a documented repair.** Invalid disconnected fragments were removed and two demonstrated bad bottom planes were restored to their recorded base elevations. The operation preserves real building surfaces and legitimate lower levels; terrain is still an open surface, not a fabricated underground solid. The [geometry audit](revamp/Source-elevation-repair-audit.json) and [isolated before-and-after render](../renders/revamp/source-elevation-repair-qa.png) document that distinction. A viewer should not expose stray source fragments as if they were actual basements.

**Native detail remains valuable.** Exchange District preserves City facade textures and geometry, with completed EXS separated from the remaining development. Promenade and Voya use source-backed balcony contours with illustrative slab treatment. M3's crown terraces, Voya's shorter height and some concept envelopes retain stated uncertainty. Voya's shared podium and exact M5 architecture remain absent. The Voya review also corrected coplanar roof caps and checked every upper-cap triangle's orientation without changing its external outline or maximum height.

**Comparisons keep the camera fixed and the dates explicit.** Existing context, recorded construction and retained development serve different purposes. Recorded construction now uses dated structural evidence: M4 is clipped to an approximate level-41 envelope, M3 includes its topped silhouette, Voya shows its labelled topped envelopes and Exchange EX1/EX2 use selected unchanged native triangles. EX3 and M5 lack defensible absolute construction-height cuts, so their full planned envelopes are hidden in that mode. A gap is preferable to displaying a future roof as an observed construction stage. None of these choices makes the mixed-date municipal baseline a September 2026 as-built survey.

**Colour communicates evidence.** Existing context is neutral, construction blue, proposals rose and unresolved versions or uncertain proxies violet. Exchange's preserved source textures are an exception to the plain palette. The Hurontario LRT remains a visible route feature. Violet means that the source or reconstruction has a stated uncertainty, not that the depicted form is certain to be built.

The transit refinement includes mapped rail and selected platform geometry, plus schematic trains, canopies and street furniture. The vehicles illustrate the future line and do not imply passenger service is operating. The downtown extension remains separate from the core project. Georeferenced source context and modelled display objects are identified separately in the provenance files.

The pictures are composed architectural model views, not photographs or photorealistic forecasts. Their visual quality does not establish complete coverage, independent corroboration of every dimension or final approval of every proposal. The [model guide](Model-guide.md), [project register](project-register.csv), [refinement register](../data/refinement-models.json) and [accuracy audit](Accuracy-and-coverage-audit.md) explain the remaining limits.
