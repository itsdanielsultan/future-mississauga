# New reference review · 29 September 2026

This is a targeted update to Future Mississauga using newly supplied project references. It does not mean every development has been rechecked on this date. Proposed projects may change or never proceed.

## Cooksville GO transit-oriented community

The previous seven approximate tower envelopes are replaced by the supplied Arup Step 4 design geometry: seven towers, connected podium arrangements, setbacks, courtyard edges and rooftop structures. The August 17, 2026 submission cover letter and September 2026 draft planning report agree with the September 22 public presentation: **seven towers, 32–49 storeys and 3,171 homes**. Older labels remaining in some drawings are not treated as the current proposal.

Source coordinates were transformed to the atlas system. Underground parking was removed from the display geometry using the atlas terrain. That terrain and its alignment to design elevations are approximate; this is not a survey or fabrication model. Final facade finishes, construction start and completion remain unconfirmed. November 2026 is a planned submission milestone, not an occupancy date.

[Infrastructure Ontario project and public documents](https://www.infrastructureontario.ca/en/what-we-do/projectssearch/cooksville-go-transit-oriented-community/) · [September 22 public presentation](https://www.infrastructureontario.ca/4a7380/contentassets/48d409806ba14ffaa6f9874dcf14617e/cooksville_voh-presentation_sept-4-2026.pdf)

## Clarkson GO transit-oriented community

The previous traced envelopes are replaced by the supplied Zeidler design massing dated November 28, 2025. It contains the seven towers, three podiums and courtyard forms. The January 2026 presentation identifies **2,434 homes and 25–45 storeys**. This is schematic architectural massing, not a finished facade model.

The native file has no verified geographic or elevation datum. It is registered to the existing station-based atlas placement at its original scale, with approximate terrain grades. Podium foundations are extended slightly below the terrain to prevent visible gaps. A1's elevation sheet says 87.75 m while the site plan and supplied model use 82.75 m. The native model is retained and the conflict is disclosed rather than inventing an extra rooftop cap. No completion year is confirmed.

## South Banks

The original high-resolution artist concept and elevation reference support repeated cross-gables on nine of the seventeen townhome blocks. Those roof shapes have been added while preserving block placement, footprints and overall heights. Their dimensions are inferred; no native architectural model or approved metric roof dimensions were supplied. The advertised **2029 occupancy** is a marketing target, not a guarantee or verified construction stage.

[DECO Homes](https://www.decohomes.ca/southbanks/) · [Public launch information, secondary source](https://www.southbanks-mississauga.com/)

## Other catalogue corrections

- **Square One Block 22:** the City's applications remain open but the applicant has put the project on hold. The retained 55/65-storey scheme is credited to Hariri Pontarini Architects, drawings reissued April 23, 2021. No construction timeline is assigned.
- **6020 Winston Churchill:** the 2023 four-storey, 20-unit approval is distinguished from the later 22-unit rental programme. The former architect is a historical reference; a successor is not verified. Conflicting height figures from different design stages have not been used to rescale the model. The construction period is a programme forecast; an actual start has not been independently confirmed.
