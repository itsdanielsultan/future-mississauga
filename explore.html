      About the 3D map

      The map combines City of Mississauga 3D data with building studies and project research. Source dates vary; proposals and building shapes may change.

      Choose a building to see its details and sources.

      The city overview uses fewer small buildings. Choose an area for neighbourhood detail. Roofs, terrain and tree sizes are approximate.

      Transit lines show illustrative routes and levels, not engineering alignments. The Hazel McCallion Line’s core includes Brampton; this map shows its Mississauga section. The downtown loop is a separate extension in planning and design. Source dates and model limits are recorded in Sources.

      This is independent work by Daniel Sultan. It is not an official City plan or a notice that a property will be demolished.
