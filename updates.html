More of the city.

I checked the City’s 72 active application pages, recent planning notices, project-team references and construction updates against the models. Here’s what I added or corrected.

These changes are in the interactive model. The existing gallery and picture atlas remain dated renders; they don’t automatically change when new evidence arrives.

New planning decisions.

The October 8 notices record September 29 passage for 2155 Leanne Boulevard, 1970–1980 Fowler Drive, 1225 Dundas East, 6344 Ninth Line, 3115 Hurontario, 44–45 Longview Place. The appeal periods remain open through October 28. Some sites also retain holding conditions. These decisions are not proof that construction has begun.

Birch’s site-plan approval is now recorded separately from its unverified construction status. A City-owned Kariya parkland parcel has also been designated as public open space; that change does not alter the proposed Exchange condo towers.
