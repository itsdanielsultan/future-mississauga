MODEL AND EVIDENCE REVIEW · 12 OCTOBER 2026

More of the city.

I checked the City’s 72 active application pages, recent planning notices, project-team references and construction updates against the models. Here’s what I added or corrected.

These changes are in the interactive model. The existing gallery and picture atlas remain dated renders; they don’t automatically change when new evidence arrives.

October 12: the latest checks.

New planning and construction evidence is now recorded, alongside the sites that still need reliable architecture. A locator marks a researched project; it does not stand in for a finished building model.
