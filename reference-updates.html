Building reference updates.

I reached out to photographers, designers and artists to help make these buildings and landmarks more accurate.

These are approximate exterior studies. Original photographs and drawings stay private. If you have a useful drawing, photo or 3D file, I’d love to hear from you at daniel@futuremississauga.com.

These render studies show the exterior details I could add from shared references, with earlier versions where available. Winding Trail and 228 Queen Street South are now also in the city model. Other studies remain separate where their placement or details need more checking.

Zonta Meadows Park · Artwork study

Gala Gateway

Gabriel Fain pointed me to photographs of Gala Gateway, the artwork he created with Harley Valentine. I used them to study its steel frame and two granite stones, then sent these views back for feedback. He has given permission to share them here and in the atlas.

The shape and size are approximate. I haven't confirmed the artwork's exact position or direction in Zonta Meadows Park, so these views use a simple display base. It has not been placed in the 3D city map. Gala Procession is a separate artwork.

Original artwork: Harley Valentine and Gabriel Fain. Model study: Future Mississauga. Original reference photographs remain private.

The exterior references shared by KMA helped me add the tower, glazed front bays and lower green roofs that were missing from the City model. The earlier model and updated study use the same viewpoints.

The City footprint provides the starting scale and shape. Tower height, roof levels, facade details and unseen rear surfaces are estimates. The plain surrounding ground is for display only.

Exterior reference material: Kleinfeldt Mychajlowycz Architects (KMA). Starting building geometry: City of Mississauga.

Cooksville · Updated exterior study

2200 Sherobee Road

I used Panoramic Properties’ photographs to refine the northwest side facing Sherobee Road and correct the oversized rooftop block. These matched views compare the City’s original model with the new study, both up close and among the surrounding buildings.

The rooftop enclosure keeps its original mapped footprint, with its height estimated at 4.5 m above the main roof. Roof height, balcony depth and entrance details are photo-derived approximations; measured dimensions and hidden rear finishes remain unverified.

Reference photographs supplied by Panoramic Properties. Starting building and surroundings: City of Mississauga. Road-direction cross-check: OpenStreetMap contributors.
