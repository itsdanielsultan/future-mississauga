# Lakeview Village: current model coverage

Reviewed September 27, 2026. This edition includes a new, approximate Harbourwalk reconstruction, the retained municipal massing and ten source-plan Aura envelopes. It does not reconstruct the complete future village. Unresolved phases are kept in the research catalogue without filling their parcels with historical or invented towers.

The [City overview](https://www.mississauga.ca/projects-and-strategies/city-projects/lakeview-waterfront-area-redevelopment/lakeview-village/) describes 12,000 homes, while the [developer's current page](https://mylakeviewvillage.com/project) advertises up to 11,000. These programme descriptions differ from the provincially permitted ceiling of 16,000; the illustration does not claim one resolved final count. [September reporting](https://thepointer.com/article/2026-09-04/mississauga-residents-skeptical-after-lakeview-village-proposal-drops-by-4k-units-to-accommodate-more-townhomes) identifies apartment-to-townhouse revisions on blocks 4, 8 and 9: the block numbers come through the local ratepayer association, while the article quotes the City on the three-block change. No complete, current, dimensioned masterplan was recovered in this review.

## Harbourwalk: added reference reconstruction

Harbourwalk's distinctive form was absent from the previous model. The new asset reconstructs its **two 19-storey wings, descending terraces, projecting balcony bands and open courtyard** from [Tridel's current exterior references](https://www.tridel.com/lakeviewvillagecondos/) and its [published brochure](https://www.landpower.ca/static/media/Harbourwalk-Brochure.pdf). The brochure is a 2022 marketing document, cross-checked against the current imagery; it is not a dimensioned permit drawing.

The form is registered to the public City application parcel for **SP 21/168**, approved **January 15, 2025**, at **910 Aerodrome Avenue / 700 Leading Sea Avenue**. The [City site-plan service](https://services6.arcgis.com/hM5ymMLbxIyWTjn2/ArcGIS/rest/services/Site_Plan_Applications/FeatureServer/0) supports the parcel and two 19-storey buildings. Approximate image registration supplies orientation and scale. Working allowances are ±5 m placement and ±3 m outline, not survey confidence intervals.

The displayed **61.7 m maximum is estimated**, comprising an approximately 60.8 m roof and 0.9 m parapet. No official metre height was recovered; the working height allowance is ±6 m. The 5 m entrance level, 3.10 m upper-floor spacing, terrace depths, slab/railing thicknesses and facade details are inferred. Its 240 components are model parts, not separate buildings. No source photograph or brochure artwork is embedded as a model texture.

Harbourwalk appears in the **future scenario only**. [Tridel's August 2026 construction update](https://www.tridel.com/lakeviewvillagecondos/news/) confirms construction, but does not establish measured partial-building heights. The planned finished model must not be read as the present construction stage. Its foundation meets the sampled terrain; future civil grading is not reconstructed.

The native-source search found no matching Harbourwalk component among the retained 153,021 municipal records or the checked public scene resources. The new footprint was compared with all 26 retained Lakeview source components and the ten Aura envelopes: there was no overlapping projected area, and none was removed. This is a bounded audit, not a claim that an architect's native model cannot exist elsewhere.

## Aura, other phases and historical plans

Aura has **ten approximate envelopes** in the future scenario, labelled uncertain. Seven use the recovered [June 2026 site-plan evidence](https://caivan.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Caivan-Aura-Site-Plan-260615.pdf), and three follow the other image linked from [the developer's current page](https://caivan.com/aura/). An image filename alone does not establish its revision date. The 13 m display height is an illustrative proportion estimate with ±3.5 m working allowance; placement and roof outlines remain approximate. Advertised two-storey residences describe portions of the building and do not establish a two-storey total height.

[Pier House](https://www.branthaven.com/communities/pier-house/) is a current three-storey, non-stacked townhouse offering, but a sufficiently registered footprint/elevation set was not recovered. Its offering alone does not prove that the older Birch condominium proposal was cancelled or replaced. [DECO/OPUS](https://opushomes.com/communities/lakeview-village/), the Innovation District and other village blocks also need better parcel-specific references. Sales-gallery addresses are not building coordinates.

The developer still links [Development Master Plan 4.0](https://cdn.aglty.io/tgbxpvs6/pillars/planning/LakeviewVillage_DMP4.0.pdf), dated October 2019. The [environmental noise report](https://www.mississauga.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/01115530/lakeview-village-environmental-noise-feasibility-report.pdf) is dated September 14, 2022 despite its 2024 upload path; page 44 uses Sasaki massing received August 16, 2022. Both are useful historical context. Neither justifies filling every missing parcel with those older towers or treating their storey labels as current approval.

The new Harbourwalk and earlier Aura additions use source-referenced shapes with explicit uncertainty. They do not turn the historical masterplan into a supposedly complete current model. When better current plans or licensed native files become available, the corresponding phase can be revised without inventing a definitive final village.

## Timing and landscape

Current Tridel suite pages for [2K](https://www.tridel.com/condos/lakeviewvillagecondos/1678227525/) and [2M+D](https://www.tridel.com/condos/lakeviewvillagecondos/1716401810/) advertise **Fall 2028 occupancy, subject to change**. This replaces the earlier Summer 2028 forecast in the project record. The [City district-energy overview](https://www.mississauga.ca/projects-and-strategies/city-projects/lakeview-waterfront-area-redevelopment/lakeview-village/) separately anticipates initial occupied buildings in early 2029. The sources may describe different milestones; neither is an actual completion date or a whole-community completion promise.

Lakeview ground uses the provincial DTM and municipal planimetric outlines. The present Jim Tovey reclamation edge remains incompletely verified: older municipal boundaries and a broad CVC construction polygon cannot replace a current surveyed shoreline. Its public park opening on May 30, 2026 is a separate milestone. [CVC opening announcement](https://cvc.ca/discover-the-new-jim-tovey-lakeview-conservation-area/).
