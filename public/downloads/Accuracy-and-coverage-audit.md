# Accuracy and coverage audit

Research date: 20 September 2026; geometry and version review updated 21 September 2026. Transit Revamp edition. Current building additions, replacement controls and totals are in [refinement-models.json](../data/refinement-models.json), [stats.json](../data/stats.json) and the [refinement notes](Refinement-notes.md). This review compares the three research dossiers, component overrides, the downtown geometry audit, the complete municipal-group audit and the exported research register. It does not certify a survey, construction drawing or completed architectural model.

## Assessment

The refinement edition adds source-derived architecture to the citywide municipal massing atlas. M3 now includes a stepped crown silhouette, M5 has a native developer interaction proxy, and Voya has two curved plan-derived tower envelopes. Square One Block 5 T1, the Promenade infill, Cooksville and Clarkson TOCs, and several revised waterfront buildings also have new models. The current register contains 24 supplemental assets. The Transit Revamp also adds the corrected Absolute World pair and plan-derived envelopes for Bough Beeches, Chelsea B4/B5/B6 and 55 Dundas. Assets can represent a building part, multiple buildings or a context feature, so this total is not a building count. Street and transit detail assets are documented separately.

**The model still does not provide complete, current architectural geometry for every future Mississauga building.** Some additions use dimensioned plans, while others use approximate traces, dated concepts, photo-derived silhouettes or an unverified developer proxy. Their different evidence levels are recorded individually. The newer geometry reduces important gaps without converting unknown dimensions or construction stages into verified facts.

The baseline also needs a date qualification: municipal capture dates vary, including downtown 2024 LiDAR. A current research status cannot make an older construction capture into a September 2026 as-built model.

## Geometry corrections in the Transit Revamp

The Absolute World replacement preserves mapped placement and the shared podium while restoring the distinct 56- and 50-storey forms at source-supported heights of 175.6 m and 157.9 m. The architectural floorplate and rotation anchors constrain the model, but intermediate twist interpolation and facade treatment remain approximations. The adjacent source buildings at 90 and 76 Absolute are separate buildings; their invalid deep bottom planes were corrected without rescaling their towers.

A citywide elevation audit found disconnected, invalid low-altitude fragments in 301 City source records. The corrected exports remove exactly 8,450 fragment triangles. Two additional records had connected bottom planes tens of metres below their own recorded base elevation; only those planes were restored to the municipal BASEELEV, affecting 66 triangles. All legitimate building surfaces and basement levels remain. The original City files are preserved as raw evidence. This is a source-defect repair, not a global terrain clip or an as-built basement reconstruction. The [repair audit](revamp/Source-elevation-repair-audit.json) records exact files, source IDs, checksums and validation, and the [isolated comparison](../renders/revamp/source-elevation-repair-qa.png) shows the raw forms on the left and corrected forms on the right.

Separate B3 (73-4292) stays visible in violet: its old municipal envelope is 52.1 m, while the current phase 2 site plan labels B3 as 25 storeys under a separate application. No unverified conversion from storeys to metres was applied. The new B4/B5/B6 envelopes remain separate proposals. ## Work still needed for a complete current model

The remaining geometric work is specific: replace M3/M4 schematic details and the M5 interaction proxy with fully dimensioned current architecture; establish Voya's exact shorter height, podium and floor-by-floor geometry; resolve remaining phase mapping at Square One, Brightwater and Lakeview; recover replacements for still-excluded revised schemes; update Cooksville to the latest drawing set; and reconcile roof-height conventions and ground levels. Final geometry should then be checked against at least two suitable sources, including an authoritative drawing or approval where available, and compared with current construction photography for completed buildings.

The uncertain M5 proxy overlaps the older municipal capture at 3971 Redmond Road (63-2901), about 540 m² of roof in the current registration. Exact demolition or site-boundary evidence has not been established for this individual captured form, so it remains rather than being deleted using the uncertain proxy alone. The [model-layer audit](revamp/Model-layer-audit.json) records this remaining overlap.

The accurate description is a researched city model combining municipal captures, native textured designs and documented plan-derived or illustrative supplements, with explicit gaps and version controls. Claims of absolute accuracy, complete current architecture, or independent corroboration of every record would overstate the delivered evidence.


## September 21 route-visibility correction

The coloured routes are **cartographic annotations**: GO stays green, the Transitway blue and HML gold. The dedicated busway and its current street/highway connection use the same blue, with their meaning retained in segment labels. The 3 m guide strips have uniform unlit colours and are separate from grey physical tracks. Their horizontal paths are unchanged. A separately disclosed upper display profile and an 8% visualization slope cap keep the guides clear of checked ground, road, bridge and platform surfaces. Original physical profiles retain their earlier 3.5% GO, 6% HML and 8% bus display caps. Neither set of values is measured engineering.

The surface audit checks the actual guide centres and both edges at no more than 1 m spacing: 509,181 samples, including 508,179 with an overlapping context surface, and no remaining surface-contact risks. The audit covers 77 source files. The browser and rendered pictures treat the guide as a cartographic overlay, so it may draw across a foreground building in an oblique view. This does not imply a railway above a roof or a changed geographic alignment. Physical rails retain normal depth and remain below ground where the schematic profile is below grade. Composed pictures use full municipal transit assets; ordinary district windows label continuations at their clip boundaries.
