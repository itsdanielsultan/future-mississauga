# How to read the Mississauga model

21 November 2026 · Daniel Sultan

The model puts proposed buildings beside the streets, homes and towers already around them. Switch between the three stages from the same viewpoint.

| Stage | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Buildings recorded as existing or in use. |
| Construction | Existing buildings plus construction for which the model has dated physical evidence. |
| Future | Existing buildings alongside the planned shapes of projects. Plans can change. |

## Colours

| Colour | Project status |
| --- | --- |
| Grey | Existing or completed |
| Blue | Under construction |
| Pink | Proposed or approved. Each project record distinguishes the two. |
| Violet | Status or current proposal uncertain |

On the interactive map, select **Planning status** to see these colours. Roof caps, balconies and glass can retain their architectural colours. Each tower in a development can be at a different stage. At Exchange District, EX1, EX2 and EXS are in use and EX3 is under construction.

## Where the detail comes from

The City’s building layer was updated in June 2026 and uses a 2024 laser survey. More recent planning records, construction reports, drawings and photographs inform individual projects. Some heights, roof shapes and façades are estimates. An announcement that construction has started does not establish how many floors have been built.
