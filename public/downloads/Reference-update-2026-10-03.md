# Future Mississauga: community corrections, 3 October 2026

Block Nine's completed North and South towers at **4085 and 4055 Parkside Village Drive** have been added to the model, including their connected podium. The City identifies the North tower as **25 storeys** and the South tower as **34 storeys**. Both appear in existing, construction and future scenarios because they are existing buildings.

The shared base follows the City's 2024 mapped building outlines. The original developer brochure guides tower placement and architectural character. The approximately **79.9 m and 106.5 m** vertical envelopes come from a secondary catalogue; they are not independently verified as-built heights. Podium height, balcony projections, facade fins and roof enclosures are simplified estimates. No exact completion day or architect-native model was recovered. Secondary sources report completion in 2020.

The newly identified address was a real gap in the inherited model. The current City LOD2 scene was also checked around the site before making the reference-derived addition. Existing neighbouring geometry is retained.

## Earlier feedback checked again

- **EX3:** the existing shorter 66-storey interpretation and blue crown are retained. The current architect page still describes 66 storeys. Its exact revised metre height remains unverified.
- **Avia and Voya:** both pairs are present. Avia is completed; Voya's developer still advertises Fall 2026 completion, which remains a forecast. Voya's connected, curved podium and source-traced balcony profiles are retained.
- **15, 35 and 65 Watergarden Drive:** Gemma and both Perla towers are present. Perla's approximate heights and details remain disclosed.

The original Reddit gallery shows an earlier snapshot. Current model views and dated source notes are the best place to check subsequent revisions. Proposed developments elsewhere remain possibilities rather than guarantees.
