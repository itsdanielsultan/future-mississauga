# Community reference and coverage review

September 26, 2026. This review covers all **108 named project dossiers**, including the added Perla record. It does not mean that every municipal building component was researched independently. The City’s native 3D dataset remains the broad geographic baseline; one GLB or component can contain several towers, and one building can have several components.

The initial availability pass checked 127 unique cited pages or files across the original 107 dossiers and retrieved 123. It found no direct exterior 3D download link in those inspected pages. That is a bounded search result, not proof that no model exists elsewhere. Automatic matches for “drawings” or “renderings” can include unrelated page navigation and were not counted as verified project plans. Focused primary-source checks and professional reference requests followed.

## Changes supported by the focused review

- **15, 35 and 65 Watergarden Drive:** Gemma’s full supplied tower and podium are used for the occupied phase, with its incomplete construction capture removed as a duplicate. The two Perla towers were absent from seven inspected native scene leaves and receive approximate envelopes based on City plans and the mapped footprint. Their exact architectural metre heights, balconies and roof details remain unverified. Gemma’s move-in evidence does not complete the three later southern SP 22-99 towers; those four tower/podium components are explicitly Proposed.
- **Parkside:** both Avia towers occur within one City component; Voya already has two tower-body supplements. Additional duplicate towers were not invented. The later Block 11 phase is a separate reference scope. Missing scene links were restored so the retained shapes are visible in the published district models.
- **Cooksville GO TOC:** Infrastructure Ontario’s September 22, 2026 open-house deck has seven towers, replacing the older eight-tower arrangement. The current envelopes follow that layout and storey counts. Heights are illustrative estimates, with the omission of podia, facade detail and rooftop plant disclosed. The announced November 2026 submission is not a completion date.
- **Exchange EX3:** the separate native-model correction removes the upper black module and lowers the original crown onto the retained white roof. Other phase geometry and texture are preserved. The model-derived vertical change is an inference; no revised official metre height or completed blue crown is claimed.
- **Erin Mills Town Centre:** the separate current-settlement review replaces the old arrangement with nine towers of 25, 25, 20, 20, 23, 23, 23, 35 and 37 storeys. The June 2026 settlement drawing supports this arrangement; estimated metre heights and placement limits are recorded with the model.
- **Kariya, Sherwoodtowne and older master plans:** proposals and unresolved versions remain distinguishable from existing buildings. Age alone is not treated as cancellation. Downtown21 is a 2010 policy/vision source, not a current architectural approval for every illustrated building. The City’s latest Living Arts vision page expressly says final design, funding and construction are not approved.

## Reference and permission limits

All 108 dossiers now have a verified sent reference request or referral in the reference-request review: 98 are scoped to publicly identified professional project teams, one to the City’s named Living Arts project lead, and nine to a consolidated City request to identify current reference holders. The last nine are routing requests, not contact with a confirmed model owner. Actual sent messages are matched by their named project scope; a message about Avia and Voya does not automatically cover all Amacon projects. Bounced addresses are excluded from nonfailed-route coverage, and rerouted requests remain distinct attempts. A sent request does not establish receipt, a delivered model, or permission to publish it.

No newly requested native model or new reuse permission is claimed in this audit. Public drawings and brochure images remain their owners’ material. Requests distinguish permission for published renders from permission for a lightweight public web 3D view, and offer credit. Professional contact details and private correspondence are kept out of the public reference JSON.

The 40 MW Tenth Line data-centre record already carries the City’s September 16, 2026 temporary-pause status. It has no retained geometry in the reviewed model. The pause is not recorded as cancellation. The current 1225 Dundas record already reflects the 16-storey revision and excludes unavailable current-design geometry.
