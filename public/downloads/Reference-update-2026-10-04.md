# Evidence update: 4 October 2026

The city is a researched scenario, not a forecast or a guarantee that every proposal will be built. Current delivery status and model detail have separate evidence.

## Delivery status

**7055 Tranmere Drive.** The project architect reported in October that the PMLA scheme is not proceeding. Its proposed warehouse geometry is omitted from the future view; the existing frontage building is retained. The municipal inventory still lists SP 21-161 as Withheld. This project-team update is not proof of formal municipal cancellation.

**6620 Rothschild Trail.** The earlier PMLA condominium scheme is not proceeding and its superseded geometry remains omitted. The newer RN Design proposal for 17 townhouses is a separate version and remains in the research register. No cancellation of that newer application has been established. The [City application page](https://www.mississauga.ca/services-and-programs/planning-and-development/development-applications/active-development-applications/6620-rothschild-trail-oz-opa-19-10-w11/) and [February 2025 site plan](https://www.mississauga.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/13110909/Site-Plan-Concept-OZ-19-10-W11-February-2025.pdf) document the revised layout.

Municipal application status was checked against the [City active development inventory](https://services6.arcgis.com/hM5ymMLbxIyWTjn2/arcgis/rest/services/Growth_Management_%E2%80%93_Active_Development_Applications/FeatureServer/0/query?where=SiteAddress%20LIKE%20%27%25Tranmere%25%27%20OR%20SiteAddress%20LIKE%20%27%25Rothschild%25%27&outFields=*&outSR=4326&f=json). An open or Withheld application is not a construction or completion date.

## New reference material

[Bloom Architects](https://www.bloomarchitects.ca/) supplied a partial residential façade study described as dating from 2018 and confirmed public Future Mississauga viewer use with Bloom Architects credit. The original file and a private local conversion are preserved. Historical City mapping and current parcel records corroborate the property’s identity; this does not verify the model’s position or orientation in the city scene. Current exterior references differ from the supplied design. City placement and current-building integration remain unverified, so the model remains a private historical design reference and has not replaced the existing building. The original files, private correspondence and local derivatives are not published.

M3’s source record now describes the separate crown silhouette visible in the current model. It remains a photo-and-plan-derived approximation, not a completed-building survey. Newly collected Westminster drawings and other public planning PDFs provide design references; older brochures, marketing targets and archive photographs have not been promoted to current completion evidence.

Other new replies offer elevations, require owner consent, or propose licensed photographs. An offer, an automated acknowledgement or a public brochure does not establish receipt of a complete model, publication permission or current completion. Private correspondence and original supplied files are not published with this update.
