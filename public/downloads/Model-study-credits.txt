Artform
86 Dundas Street East · Approximate exterior · Rev03
Model study: Daniel Sultan / Future Mississauga. Architectural design: Studio JCI. Developer: EMBLEM. Reference photographs: Edward Skira and Tim MacDonald / UrbanToronto; Google Street View, April 2026.
A completed-building exterior study informed by historical plans and dated photographs. April 2026 observations inform the warm masonry, frontage openings and recessed colonnade. Rev03 removes only exact duplicate opaque surfaces.
Passage depth, rear continuation, hidden faces and finished heights remain unverified. Zoning maxima are not measured as-built heights. Ground, interiors and surrounding site works are excluded; this standalone study is not a surveyed placement on the city map.
Independent study; no architect, owner or photographer endorsement. Model geometry and generated views are separate from the architectural design and reference-image rights. Original photographs, Google image pixels and planning PDFs are not included.
