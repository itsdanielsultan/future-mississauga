# 30 Eglinton Avenue West

Reviewed September 27, 2026.

The future model includes three approved development envelopes of 29, 36 and 31 storeys, with 1,432 homes. The original taller municipal design stays excluded. The existing office building and Wilcox House are retained; low retail wings are removed only in the future scenario. Existing and construction views retain their original context.

## What the approvals establish

The November 2023 settlement defines the revised development. The January 2024 order grants final official-plan and zoning approval. The May 2026 order corrects interim commercial-use provisions. The June 2026 consolidated RA5-62 schedule supplies the mapped envelopes and maximum heights of 93.5, 113.1 and 100 metres.

No construction start, final site-plan approval, holding-condition removal or completion date has been verified. The City workflow label “Withheld” is not proof of cancellation. Earlier 28/32/34 and 45/45/37 descriptions are superseded.

## Model limits

Approved settled-development envelope reconstruction, not final architectural/BIM geometry. The November 2023 settlement describes 29, 36 and 31 storeys and 1,432 dwellings; the June 2026 consolidated RA5-62 schedule supplies dimensional and height limits. The earlier 45/45/37 City assembly remains excluded. Printed tower plates and step heights are explicit; outer podium contours and positions are independently reconstructed to City parcel 5263100. The connected A3/A4 collar is interpreted at A3 32.1 m and the connected C3/C4 collar at C3 32.5 m; these collar heights are not separately labelled in the schedule. C podium frontage is traced approximately around the bend; minor setbacks, facade, balconies, mechanical enclosures and approved grading are not reconstructed. Display foundations bridge the Ontario DTM and do not establish the legal architectural height datum. Placement ±2 m, podium outline ±1 m and grade ±1.5 m are modelling judgements, not surveyed tolerances. Zoning approval and an active application do not establish construction, final site-plan approval or a completion date. In the future view only, exact municipal office components are retained while overlapping low retail wings are removed. The existing and construction views preserve the original office and retail context. Wilcox House remains unchanged.
