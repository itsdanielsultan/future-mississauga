# Sources for the Mississauga model

21 November 2026 · Daniel Sultan

## Buildings

The City of Mississauga supplies the base building shapes. Its June 2026 release uses a 2024 laser survey. Planning applications, architectural drawings and dated construction evidence inform the more recent projects. Open a building record for its architect, developer, sources and known estimates. [Building records](../accuracy-review.html) · [Model guide](Model-guide.md)

## Reading the map

| Colour | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Grey · `#b8b9b6` | Existing buildings. Taller buildings have darker faces to make the skyline easier to read. |
| Blue · `#85b4bf` | Under construction. |
| Pink · `#d8b7ac` | Proposed or approved. The building record gives its specific stage. |
| Violet · `#b89bc3` | Uncertain project or design. |

Choose **Planning status** in the 3D map to see the key. Greens identify parks and trees, blue-grey marks water, and transit routes have their own line colours. Shadows and architectural finishes affect how individual surfaces look. The compass points north for the camera angle shown. [Open the map](../explore.html?appearance=status)

## Using a view

**Copy view link** on the interactive map saves the camera position, target, area and scenario. A map PNG also stores the view link and its original scene dimensions in its metadata. The three Downtown skyline pictures use the same camera, so you can compare buildings in the same spot. For model changes, the before-and-after studies retain their original camera angles. [Compare Downtown](../compare.html) · [Building revisions](../reference-updates.html) · [City camera settings](City-view-cameras-20261121.json) · [Study camera settings](Building-revision-cameras-20261121.json)
