# Lakeview refinement assets

These two optional supplements use the atlas coordinate system and can be loaded without changing its terrain. Their complete sources, bounds, checksums and display modes are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

- **Aura townhome envelopes:** ten approximate buildings, comprising seven footprints highlighted in the developer’s June 2026 plan and three additional blocks highlighted in the currently linked image. Their placement does not overlap any of the 26 original City Lakeview model components. The closest conservative City component boundary is 68.1 m away. Use only in the future view, with the **Uncertain** colour/status.
- **Mapped waterfront:** thirteen closed pier/deck polygons formed from the City’s 2024 linework, plus one complete OpenStreetMap sand-beach polygon near Lakefront Promenade. Use as existing scenery in all modes, preserving the supplied materials.

## Accuracy of the Aura envelopes

The complete June drawing is registered to two exact City street junctions. Four shared roof centroids transfer the newer image into that registration, with 0.55–1.48 m residuals. These are agreement measures between drawings, not survey confidence bounds. The printed scale bar in the newer image differs by about 25% from City junction spacing, so it does not control model scale. The declared interpretive tolerances are approximately ±10 m position and ±3 m outline.

Each envelope is **13 m tall, estimated with ±3.5 m uncertainty**. This comes from roughly 6.2 m registered unit-bay width and the approximate 2.1:1 facade-height-to-bay-width proportion in Caivan’s official illustration. It is not a published architectural dimension. Official Echo/Halo sheets show the distinction between the lower and upper dwellings; “two-storey living” describes each dwelling, not the total building. Roofs, balconies, basements and facade articulation are omitted. The seven June footprints remain visible as grey roofs in the newer marketing image, but exact revised drawings were not available. The image filename suggests August 2026; it has no visible issue date. No completion date or measured construction progress is asserted.

## Accuracy of the waterfront scenery

Pier plan outlines are extracted only where City linework closes without added edges. Heights and 0.25 m deck thickness are display approximations: median mixed-date Ontario DTM plus a small offset, with a minimum above the atlas water surface. The beach uses its actual mapped polygon, triangulated at up to 10 m edges and draped over the DTM. It is not a surveyed September 2026 waterline. The scenery does not replace the shoreline, cut the terrain, or depict new structures as already built.

**Still omitted:** Pier House’s exact site footprint and current roof/height geometry; proposed Cultural Pier renovation, new transient marina, boardwalk and park furniture. Branthaven’s production page supports the three-storey housing types but does not provide a recoverable registered footprint plan. Its sales-gallery address is not used as a building location. Park and pier concept drawings are not sufficient to label those features built.

All vertices are finite. Solid envelopes and pier decks have outward winding and positive volume. The beach is intentionally an open surface. No original municipal component is removed by these supplements.
