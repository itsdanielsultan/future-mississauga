About these pictures

22 portraits were rendered on 22 October 2026. Two earlier Clarkson views remain unchanged and do not include the latest model updates. Each picture shows its render date.

The presentation copies add a consistent status key, camera-calibrated north and creator/source credits. Geometry, materials and original render dates are unchanged. Original images and dates.

Future views show a possible outcome if the included projects proceed. Proposals, heights and timing may change; source dates and model detail vary. Read the model notes.
