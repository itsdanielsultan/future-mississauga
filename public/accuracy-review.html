How I check the models.

  1. Match the correct address, footprint, orientation and current design.
  2. Check height, roof, entrance and visible exterior against the strongest available drawings, models and photographs. Identify estimates clearly.
  3. Keep project status and completion evidence separate from what an image happens to show.
  4. Check the building in its actual surroundings, including ground, roads, neighbouring buildings and transit.
  5. Keep source credits and use permissions with the model. Test the replacement and original fallback before release.
  6. Check the normal viewer's loading and navigation. Extra detail must fit its performance budget.
