# Transit depth correction · 21 September 2026

Coloured transit markings now occupy the same 3D scene as buildings, terrain and bridges. Foreground buildings correctly hide them. The former always-visible, foreground-composited route layer has been removed.

The 28 canonical transit assets retain their horizontal coordinates, faces and colours. All 810 physical mesh position/face checks are unchanged. Guide heights follow the existing route-specific physical display profiles, with minor clearance from nearby support surfaces. An overhead road or roof is not used as a reason to raise a lower route onto it. GO remains green, the Transitway blue and HML gold. Underground and underpass paths remain connected in the data and are intentionally hidden where normal 3D occlusion applies.

The source review did not establish a defensible horizontal reroute. Metrolinx's [403 construction photographs](https://www.metrolinx.com/en/discover/hazel-mccallion-line-takes-shape-over-highway-403) and [current Centre notice](https://www.metrolinx.com/en/projects-and-programs/hazel-mccallion-lrt/notices/general-construction-update-mississauga-centre) support the elevated corridor beside the existing road bridge. The [Rathburn notice](https://assets.metrolinx.com/image/upload/v1777390899/Images/Metrolinx/HML%20Hazel%20McCallion%20Line/Rathburn_Stage_4_Notice_April_2026.pdf) confirms the western terminal corridor. The [Port Credit pushbox account](https://www.metrolinx.com/en/discover/progress-on-the-hazel-mccallion-line-) confirms the route beneath the GO railway. Moving these paths onto nearby traffic lanes would misrepresent their documented routing.

Survey-grade alignment and engineering elevations remain unavailable. The second-track gaps, counterpart wye connector, flyover display level, Port Credit depth and display slope limits remain approximate. The City pavement layer is a 2024 snapshot and does not define every new reserved rail strip. No claim of final engineering geometry is made.

The staged 1600×1000 junction, Port Credit and Uptown tests use the original camera presets. They show the route behind foreground towers, the elevated HML over Highway 403, and road-level HML within the corridor. The full 33-picture batch is a separate final step.
