October model updates

Viewer and coverage update · 23 October 2026

I've made building sides and corners clearer in the 3D viewer and added closer views of Lisgar & Argentia and Meadowvale Village, with East Credit & Creditview added on October 23. The larger goal is still to improve the references and models for every structure, including older buildings.

These changes are in the interactive model. The existing gallery and picture atlas remain dated renders; they don’t automatically change when new evidence arrives.

23 October 2026 · Map coverage

A closer view of East Credit and Creditview.

The 3D map now has 20 areas, including the city overview. Its new 3.3 km by 3.2 km study retains City-source building positions and elevations, mapped streets and trees, and the GO and Transitway routes that cross it. Both Voya towers and their shared podium fit inside the study.

It reaches 4,784 additional existing source-part centres, bringing the total to 142,310, with 9,957 outside the detailed views. These are source parts, not newly verified buildings. Ground checks found 33 unsupported points on a 25 m grid; 133 unsupported tree points were omitted. Some boundary-cut project forms and source-version uncertainties remain. The gallery, atlas and film are unchanged.

The October 22 source review covers 17 proposed or construction parts across seven project families. Masjid Al Farooq describes Phase 2 as newly opened while seeking setup and finishing support; no exact opening or whole-project completion date is established for either captured part.
