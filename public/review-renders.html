About the studies

Each study includes two 2800 × 2000 pictures and a two-page PDF. The surrounding neighbourhoods come from the City’s dated mapped model. Older designs, proposals and estimates are noted with each project; these are approximate views, and plans can change.

If your building or project could be represented better, I’d love to improve it. Email daniel@futuremississauga.com. A 3D model is ideal; drawings, PDFs and clear photographs can also help.
