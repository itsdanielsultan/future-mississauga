BUILDING STUDIES

Each building, in its place.

I reached out to developers, architects and local project teams to help make my models of Mississauga more accurate. The drawings, photos and models they shared helped me improve these buildings and show how they fit into the neighbourhood.

If your building or project is shown here and could be represented better, I’d love to improve it. Email me at daniel@futuremississauga.com. A 3D model is ideal, but drawings, PDFs or clear photos can help me work from what you have.
