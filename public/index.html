About this comparison

This view compares model layers. It does not predict a completion year or indicate that a particular home will be demolished. Source capture dates vary. The project register records missing geometry and unresolved revisions.

Hurontario LRT

The gold line traces the Hazel McCallion Line through Port Credit, Cooksville, downtown and northern Mississauga. The LRT is under construction.

Route details & model limits

The 18 km, 19-stop core project includes Brampton. The model is clipped to Mississauga; the separate downtown loop remains a planning and design project. Green GO and blue Transitway guides show the other connections. Route lines sit within the 3D scene, so terrain, buildings and bridges can naturally hide the sections behind them. Their colours identify transit systems; their profiles retain the limits described in the model guide.
