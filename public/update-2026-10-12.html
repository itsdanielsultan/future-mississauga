October 12, 2026

What the latest plans actually showed

I checked the planning records behind projects in Erin Mills, Lakeview, Port Credit and other neighbourhoods.

Reading a city decision

I distinguished a site-plan approval from a construction start at Pier House. At Erin Mills Town Centre, I documented the conditional Tribunal decision and the work that still stood between that decision and construction.

The latest checks

New planning and construction evidence is now recorded, alongside the sites that still need reliable architecture. A locator marks a researched project; it does not stand in for a finished building model.

Erin Mills Town Centre — northwest phase

The Tribunal approved the nine-tower, 3,162-home settlement in principle on September 24, 2026. The illustrated 20–37-storey layout is already incorporated. The Final Order remains conditional, and holding requirements, subdivision and individual site plans still matter. The settlement adds at least 2,047 m² of non-residential space and a 4,206 m² park.

Living Arts Centre precinct vision

The City is studying a vision for 12 acres around the Living Arts Centre and Community Common. Public engagement and business-case work are planned for fall 2026 to winter 2027, with recommendations to Council in spring 2027. The Living Arts Centre remains open.

Pier House

The Block 4 plan depicts 164 townhomes in 19 rows. The City’s Section 47 site-plan record now gives September 17, 2026 approval. Construction has not been independently verified.

The model retains the 19 townhouse rows from the plan. Site-plan approval does not establish a construction start.

Lakeview DXE Club

Two mid-rise buildings of eight and twelve storeys continued under a receiver. PCL reported the 397-unit development topped off in July 2026.

The model retains the documented eight- and twelve-storey buildings. The City GIS record lists 478 units, while PCL reports 397; that difference remains noted.

2935–2955 Mississauga Road

The April 2026 resubmission proposes a 12-storey apartment with 196 homes and 15 stacked townhouses, 211 homes total. The City webpage still says 20 townhouses, but current architecture and servicing agree on 15. Four existing City source components already represent the site.

Existing City buildings are already shown. Current roof and terrace details remain to be compared with the newer plans.

Port Credit Active Transportation Bridge

A walking and cycling connection from Front Street North to Port Credit Memorial Park. The City still labels it in progress and documents the girder launch. No public opening confirmation was recovered.

The City tender confirms a 66 m clear span. Final drawings are still needed for the deck height, width, abutment positions and alignment before the bridge is modelled.
