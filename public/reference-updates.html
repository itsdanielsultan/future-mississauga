Building references · 12 October 2026

A closer look at the city.

I reached out to photographers, designers and artists to make Future Mississauga more accurate. Their references have helped me add recognizable details and colours to the buildings and places below.

These are approximate models, made from the available references. The original photographs and drawings remain private. If you recognize something that could be represented better, email daniel@futuremississauga.com—I’d love to work from a useful drawing, photo or 3D file.

clarkson

NRC Advanced Materials Research Facility

The existing laboratory now has its photographed window bands, white fins, entrance and open rooftop frame. The original City footprint, roofs and plant enclosure are preserved.

Photo-informed exterior details; pane spacing, fins, doorway and rooftop frame dimensions remain estimates. No source photographs are used as textures.

Photo reference assistance: Tom Arban Photography Inc. · Exterior reference assistance: Mark Walsh / Prelco · Existing envelope: City of Mississauga LOD2 2024 LiDAR · Architecture: Diamond Schmitt, and vertical expansion by Canada FIRST (Architecture49/HOK/WSP)

W.G. Davis Meeting Place · canopy study

The open entrance canopy and its columns are added to the existing campus, using the shared photographs and mapped roof outline.

Canopy placement, members and height are approximate. Campus roofs, plaza, roads and terrain are unchanged; current condition is not independently surveyed.

Reference photographs: Riley Snelling · Unchanged campus envelope: City of Mississauga · 2024 municipal context

Bell Gairdner Estate

The main and coach houses are restored to the city model, with source-based roof forms and photo-informed windows, shutters, dormers and entrance details.

Exact dated City surfaces retained. Added facade details are estimates; the coach roof does not resolve every photographed step-down. No demolition-complete claim is made for the omitted eastern garage.

Reference photography: Industryous Photography · Existing building surfaces: City of Mississauga

dixie applewood

West End Buddhist Temple

The temple has its photographed tiered maroon roof, porch, windows and warm wall finishes, anchored to the City outline and roof-height limit.

Photo-informed roof and exterior interpretation; fine dimensions remain estimates. This does not reproduce the Buddha sculpture. The neighbouring Chaithya is a separate unconfirmed-size study.

References: Bhante Saranapala / West End Buddhist Temple · Base outline and height: City of Mississauga
