# How the city views are drawn

21 November 2026 · Daniel Sultan

The three Downtown views use the same camera, looking across Square One at an angle. Switch between Today, Construction and Proposed to see what changes on the same streets. [See the three views](../index.html#skyline)

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Grey | Existing or completed buildings. Towers are a darker grey than the ground and nearby houses. |
| Blue | Under construction. The future view can show the planned size of a building that has just broken ground. |
| Pink | Proposed or approved. The project record gives the exact status. |
| Violet | The current status or proposal is uncertain. |

Architectural details can keep their own colours. The podium below Voya is concrete. Exchange District keeps its roof caps and the light bands between its floors. Square One Block 5 Tower 1 is pink because it is proposed.

The wide and portrait views carry the location, Daniel’s byline and a compass inside the image. The downloadable atlas includes larger plates for print. [Open the atlas](edition-20261121/Mississauga-Atlas.pdf)
