Notes on the print studies

1422 Aldo Drive

I worked from the 2019 model and 2020 renders, which may show different versions of the house. The upper flat roof is plain black following the Kalgreens team’s feedback. On 10 October I corrected the house’s orientation: the entrance and garage now face northeast toward Aldo Drive, checked against aerial imagery and the City’s roof footprint. Other finishes, final grading and the current built exterior remain approximate or unconfirmed.

Burnhamthorpe Community Centre

I checked the design against photographs of the completed building, but some roof levels, glazing and solar-panel details remain approximate. The City records completion in 2024 and a reopening event on 19 February 2024; that does not establish the exact architectural completion date. The photographs’ capture dates and finished grade still need checking.

6719 Glen Erin Drive

I used the March 2026 plan for the new 16-storey building and kept the existing 13-storey apartment. The 48.15 m figure is the last residential floor slab, not the roof, so I estimated the upper enclosures; the retained building’s roughly 39 m roof is also an estimate. Current elevations, approval and status information would help me check the facades, heights and siting.

Hazelhurst Business Park
