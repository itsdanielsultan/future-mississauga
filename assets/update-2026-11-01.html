M6–M8 plans under review

Urban Capital is reviewing plans and tenure after the October 23 sale to UC M City Inc. It says Rogers retains no ownership, financing or development role in those lands. No condo launch is imminent.

On October 29, the City confirmed it had received no revised plans and issued no building permits. The last submissions show M6 at 58 storeys and 889 homes and M7/M8 at 51 and 46 storeys, with 1,457 homes combined.
