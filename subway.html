01What has actually been studied?
2001 · HISTORICAL OPTION

City Centre was considered.

The TTC’s D3 option reached City Centre but was screened out. The later Dundas subway alternative ended at Cooksville GO. They are different studies, not today’s approved route.

TTC source
2018 · ADOPTED MASTER PLAN

Dundas selected BRT.

The City endorsed Dundas Connects with bus rapid transit. Dundas BRT remains a separate project; this illustration does not replace it.

City source
2026 · CAMPAIGN PROPOSAL

A new business case was proposed.

Bonnie Crombie’s September 24 announcement proposed examining a Kipling–Sherway–Mississauga connection, including elevated options. It did not select an alignment.

Primary announcement
02How to read this map

The city uses the atlas’s sourced building and terrain models. Future buildings are retained proposals, not a prediction that everything will be built. Building shapes and source dates vary.

The purple connection is newly authored for this exploration, inspired by the broad historical corridor and current discussion. Area labels are geographic anchors, not proposed station entrances. The Toronto end extends beyond the detailed Mississauga model.

Exact routing, intermediate stops, transfers, construction method, depth and feasibility would need a formal study. The overlay deliberately stays separate from the city’s real transport geometry.
