01Has this been considered before?

Yes. The City Centre option is shown above in the original Exhibit ES-15. It was screened out because its length and cost put it beyond short- and medium-term priorities. City staff shared public historical records that helped me trace the other options below.

DUNDAS CONNECTS · JUNE 2018

A Cooksville alternative.
A decision for BRT.

Dundas Connects examined a subway alternative ending at Cooksville GO, alongside the older 1993 route toward Dixie. The adopted master plan selected bus rapid transit.

This was a separate study. It did not approve a subway to City Centre.

The renewed discussion has two separate steps: Toronto Council requested reporting on a potential Sherway extension in March 2024; Crombie’s September 2026 campaign proposed a new business case reaching downtown Mississauga. Neither action selects the purple route I have drawn above.

02Why take another look now?

I think the useful question is how a growing downtown connects to the region. A fresh study could test that against today’s development and transit plans, rather than simply carrying forward a decision made decades ago.

A growing downtown

Mississauga’s plans concentrate new homes, employment and activity in the downtown. That makes it worth testing how many future trips a subway could serve.

City’s downtown planning work ↗

A connected transit network

The Hazel McCallion Line creates a north–south spine. Cooksville has Milton GO. The City says its downtown bus terminal is already at capacity and is studying a mobility hub with better LRT, bus and Transitway connections.

Capacity and connection study ↗

Trips across the boundary

A Line 2 extension could connect Mississauga destinations directly to Toronto’s subway. Its travel-time and transfer benefits should be compared with Dundas BRT and better GO service.

Dundas BRT project ↗

What a new study needs to test

A business case still needs to compare ridership, cost, travel time, construction impacts and alternatives. It must also test the actual route, station locations and transfers. This model cannot answer those questions on its own.
